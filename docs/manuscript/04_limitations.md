# Reproduction limits

The embedded observations are values read from published plots. The
fitted bundles use those curves, so the overlays document agreement
with the fitting targets. They do not estimate uncertainty across
colonies or independently compare the alternative biological
mechanisms. The numerical signals and deposition rates are model
quantities. A successful replay shows that the software produces its
reported pattern under the supplied assumptions [@ant-source; @saund2023].

The repository also provides a nearest-neighbor lookup demonstration
based on transcribed points from the decay experiments. The analytical
curves in the upper panel come from the parametric model.
